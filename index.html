Conference Paper  ·  IEEE – ICEESI 2026
A Scalable and Intelligent Approach to Optimising Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure
International Conference on Electrical and Electronics for Sustainable Innovations (ICEESI 2026)

Abstract— The long charging time, poor distribution of the charging stations, and high cost of installations are some of the factors that continue to discourage the mainstream adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) within rural and semi-urban locations. To address these limitations, we require a scalable and agile charging environment that would not be bound by the constraints of the fixed stations and the grids. This paper proposes a model that comprises a 15 kW mobile DC fastcharging unit, an automated battery-swapping station and IoTbased real-time monitoring to increase accessibility and reduce user downtime. The system architecture comprises a 72 V, 120 Ah on-board energy module, cloud-connected sensors used to check battery health and an AI-based demand prediction model, which is trained using random forest regression to optimize the use of the chargers. The charging characteristics, battery behavior and demand patterns were simulated to evaluate the system efficiency and operational feasibility. The results suggest that the proposed hybrid charging-swapping model reduces waiting time, improves resource utilisation, and offers better scalability compared to conventional fixed charging stations.
